About us

At Bloom and Blossom, we believe flowwers have the power to express feelings, create beautiful memories,and make every moment special. Bloom and Blossom began when our founder, Boitumelo, got tired of ordering flowers online and recieving something that looked nothing like the photo and nothing like what was actually growing in South Africa that week. She strated buying directly from Highveld growers and trying rose bunches to order from a table at the Melville Sunday market. That habit never really changed. We're a proper studio now, with a shopfront on 7th street and a small team of florist, but every arrangement still starts with the question "which roses are actually good this week?" rather than fixed catalogue. Its slower, and occationally means telling a customer we're out of colour but its why the roses last and why they look like they came from somewhere real.

We are business situated in Johannesburg and Menlyn

Johannesburg (Midrand)

Our Johannesburg location is in Midrand, halfway between Johannesburg and Pretoria. It is the closest option if you live or work in Midrand, Sandton, Centurion or the northern parts of Johannesburg.

Menlyn, Pretoria

Our second location is in Menlyn, in the east of Pretoria. It suits customers in Menlyn, Waterkloof, Brooklyn, Lynnwood and Garsfontein.
